OneBridge
A live bridge between AI assistants and an open Revit model.

An MCP server that connects Claude and Copilot Studio directly to a running Revit session — query elements, inspect relationships and parameters, run model-health checks, and compose live dashboards from the active model. Kit of Parts
A 3D configurator that takes Industrialized Construction seriously.

Interactive 3D building configurator for modular and prefabricated systems. Parts carry cost, weight, carbon, and compliance data. Run earthquake shake simulations, fire spread visualizations, crane collision checks, and sustainability metrics — all in the browser. Built against real Japanese standards: seismic grades 1/2/3, BSL fire resistance, CASBEE, Ken grid.

What's different
Not a geometry viewer — a decision tool. Every part has variants with trade-off data. The AI carbon optimiser swaps your assembly toward the lowest embodied carbon configuration automatically.

Kotoba Lab
Read Japanese, understand the kanji, remember the words.

A private, browser-based Japanese reading and kanji study workspace. Paste text or open Aozora Bunko files to analyze readability, add furigana, inspect vocabulary, save unfamiliar words, and review them with built-in spaced repetition. A 6,813-entry kanji library supports search, JLPT and stroke filters, and focused family study.

What's different
Radical Tree replays strokes and separates a kanji into its visual components. From there, browse related kanji by stroke count, shared reading, radical, or direct component and turn any filtered family into a keyboard-friendly study session.
