Compare Quiet Towns

The quiet towns with a 3D city model, within about 2 hours of Kawasaki. Pick what the land must have. Each town shows how much of its land you could live on has all of it.

The land must have

How to read it

  • Every 31 m square is tested. The table adds up the squares that pass every condition you picked.
  • Land you could live on: slope under 15°, not forest, water or road. Where a town's 3D model has no land-use record, only slope and lakes are used, so more land counts.
  • "Not known" means the town's 3D model does not have that layer. It is never counted as a pass.
  • Clear view of Mt. Fuji: nothing hides the mountain, past land, forest and buildings. From far towns the mountain is small even when clear.
  • From your home: straight-line km, and drive time without traffic where the page can reach the OpenStreetMap routing server (the public site). Set your home on a town map or the finder; it is saved only in this browser.
  • These are areas, not a ranking of towns. Open a town's map to see where the land is.
