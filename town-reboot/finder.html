Methods and sources
  • Drive is from Kawasaki Station to a point inside the town, with no traffic and no stops.
  • Train is the fastest way by local trains to the town's best station, estimated from the national rail network: track length, speed by kind of railway, a stop at each station, and 6 minutes for each change. It does not know timetables, express trains or waits on rural lines. Check the timetable before you go.
  • Sea means at least 15% of the town's land is within 2 km of the sea.
  • Hills means the land inside the town rises and falls by at least 150 m.
  • 3D city model says whether the national PLATEAU open data covers the town.
  • Akiya banks hold only a small part of the empty houses. The used-house link shows the wider market.
  • Past prices are reported transactions for land of at least 150 m². The range is the middle half of sales, not an estimate for one property.
  • The budget limit keeps a town when the low end of that range is within budget. It does not promise a property is available at that price.

Sources: 2020 Population Census (population, area). Geospatial Information Authority of Japan elevation tiles. National Land Numerical Information boundaries and rivers. OpenStreetMap roads through the OSRM routing service. National Land Numerical Information railways and riders per station (2024). PLATEAU dataset list on G-Spatial Information Center. MLIT Real Estate Information Library transaction prices (2021–2026). Price data refreshed 2026-11-20.
