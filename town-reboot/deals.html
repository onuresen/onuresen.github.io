How this first version works

This is a curated, dated evidence set—not a complete market feed and not a valuation. A place ranks only after a human-readable thesis is recorded.

  • Scenery means an advertised or observed view or setting, not merely being near water or mountains.
  • Flexibility reflects known constraints on building, commercial use, land categories and recurring ownership obligations.
  • The asking price is not the all-in cost. Construction, repair, retaining, utilities, surveys, fees and taxes can reverse the result.
  • Open the source and confirm availability before acting. Then inspect title, boundaries, planning, hazards and condition with qualified professionals.
